## 1.9.1

First release.

- Paste a wallet address or a transaction hash and follow the money hop by hop.
- Bitcoin, Ethereum and EVM chains, TRON, BNB Chain and Solana, including swaps and bridges between them.
- Where it landed: exchange deposit addresses, mixers, bridges, sanctioned and reported addresses, each with its source and confidence.
- Public clues about who runs a wallet, with a saved copy of every source.
- One-click report for the police and the exchange: PDF, plain text or transactions as CSV.
- Recovery-scam shield: check anyone who offers to get your money back.
- Watch: get told when traced funds move again.
- The Windows build: one portable `.exe`.
